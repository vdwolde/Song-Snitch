# References

Finished screens by Damir Sokolovsky ([@heydamir](https://x.com/heydamir)). Look at them (the `Read` tool shows
`.webp`) before the Visuals pass and when a reviewer needs a picture of "one bright element, quiet chrome". Take
their qualities, never their subject, palette or layout. In a vdWolde project the brand's own finished screens come
first: `Branding/screens/<slug>-dark.png` and `-light.png`.

| File | What it shows | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| [unslop-dashboard-after.webp](refs/unslop-dashboard-after.webp) | A finance dashboard with two rendered payment cards and a revenue chart drawn as soft coloured curves over a mirrored grey comparison | Quiet graphite chrome; all the colour lives in two moments, a real-looking object and a chart where every colour means a series |
| [unslop-hero-after.webp](refs/unslop-hero-after.webp) | An invoicing hero with a paper plane drawn in digits and currency signs, small notes pinned to it, and the product rising from below | The visual is made of the product's own material, money, so it is the moment and the signature at once. Text sits on calm sky, the chrome is quiet, the product view invites a scroll |
| [story-dashboard-frames.webp](refs/story-dashboard-frames.webp) | Eight frames from the story video, a vibecoded dashboard unslopping itself in about 10 seconds | Everything morphs in place in a wave, red redlines measure the slop and turn white when fixed, copy rolls letter by letter, square and cropped close. Read with [story.md](story.md) |

**Not copied** (see [README.md](README.md) § Fold table), described here so the lesson survives; the images are in the
upstream repo, `https://github.com/heydamir/unslop/tree/main/skills/unslop/references/refs`:

- `beacon-engraving.webp`: an incident-monitoring hero drawn as one blue engraving of a lighthouse on a rocky coast,
  with a serif headline in the same ink. A print style works because the whole page lives in it: one ink, one paper
  tone, the headline in the calm sky the drawing leaves for it.
- `meadow-3d.webp`: a footer as a soft 3D meadow with a few matte crystal shapes and a huge wordmark standing behind
  the hills. Friendly stylised 3D with a restrained palette; type that becomes part of the scene; quiet links on open sky.

What they share: one bright element per screen, quiet chrome around it, a visual that can be explained in one
sentence, and headlines placed on space that was left empty for them.
